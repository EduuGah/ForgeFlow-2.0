# 01 — Product Vision

## Definition

ForgeFlow is a mobile-first strength-training companion that turns workout records into useful evidence of progression.

The app should feel like a serious training tool, not a spreadsheet and not a generic social network.

## Primary audience

Primary: people focused on bodybuilding/muscle-building and regular gym training.

Secondary: general gym users who want structured logging and progression history.

## Core promise

The user should be able to:
1. create a workout;
2. start it at the gym;
3. record sets quickly;
4. finish the workout;
5. immediately understand what happened;
6. track progress over time;
7. set goals and receive useful reminders.

## Product principles

1. Recording a set must be fast.
2. The active workout is the highest-priority experience.
3. Offline use must be first-class.
4. Sync must be automatic and resilient.
5. Analytics must be based on recorded data, not invented facts.
6. Notifications should be useful, not spammy.
7. Future social features must not pollute the core training UX.
8. Health-related analysis must clearly distinguish tracking from professional medical/nutritional advice.
9. Every important user action must have deterministic business rules.
10. The product must be maintainable by humans and AI agents.

## Future direction

- AI-assisted training analysis
- nutrition analysis from exported data
- friends
- rankings
- competitions
- challenges
- achievements
- richer predictive analytics
